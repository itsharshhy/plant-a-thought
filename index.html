    You’re not alone

    If you are thinking about hurting yourself, or you’re in danger, please talk to someone now. These lines are free and confidential.

      Plant a Thought is not a crisis service and does not monitor what you write.

      Privacy

      • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. We don’t use analytics or cookies.
      • Your journal stays on this device. Solo thoughts and private notes are never uploaded. Export or delete them any time.
      • Live games send moves and the words you choose to share directly to your friend. Nothing is stored.
      • Shared gardens store what you plant (and your animal name, unless the garden is anonymous) so others with the link can see it. You can delete your own thoughts; the host can close the garden, which erases every thought in it.
      • Your device gets a random ID so you can delete your own thoughts. We only store a scrambled version of it.
